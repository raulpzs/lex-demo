About LEX

A research infrastructure for understanding de jure freedom of expression around the world.

LEX is a framework for systematic legal coding of laws affecting freedom of expression. We provide a coding tool and data for scholars, practitioners, journalists, and policymakers.

LEX allows for legislative monitoring, human-rights reporting, and public-facing research on freedom of expression through legal coding with Institutional Grammar.
